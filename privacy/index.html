1. Who we are

The Bundle Up app and the website bundleup.me are provided by SYNTHO TRADE SOLUTIONS SP. Z O.O., a company registered in Poland (“we”, “us”). We are the controller of the personal data processed through the app and this website, under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

Contact for anything privacy-related, including data protection requests: hi@bundleup.me.

2. An app for parents, not children

Bundle Up is designed for adults: parents, guardians and other caregivers. It is not meant to be used by children. To suggest an outfit, the app asks you for some information about your child, such as their first name and date of birth. Please add only children you look after, and only the information the app asks for.

We do not knowingly collect personal data directly from children. If you believe a child has set up Bundle Up on their own, contact us and we will delete the account.

4. What we don't collect

  • No advertising. Bundle Up shows no ads. In the subscription SDK (Adapty) we have turned off collection of Apple's advertising identifier (IDFA), Google's advertising ID and IP address.
  • No third-party trackers. Apart from Adapty for subscriptions, the app contains no third-party analytics, advertising or crash-reporting SDKs.
  • No precise location. The app doesn't access GPS. Coordinates come from the city you choose and are rounded to about 10 km.
  • No contacts, microphone or health data.
  • No push tokens. The current version of the app doesn't send push notifications. If we add them, we will ask for your permission first and update this policy.
  • No selling. We never sell personal data or share it for advertising.

5. Who processes your data

We use a small number of service providers. They process data only on our instructions, or, where noted, under their own privacy policies.

  • Railway (Railway Corporation) hosts our server, database and photo storage.
  • Apple provides Sign in with Apple and App Store payments. What you share with Apple is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.
  • Adapty (adapty.io) checks your subscription with the App Store and tells our server whether Premium is active. It receives your Bundle Up account number (as “user_” plus the number), purchase and renewal information from Apple, a record of when the Premium screen is shown, and basic technical information about your device and app version. Collection of advertising identifiers and IP address is turned off in the SDK.
  • Weather and place search: Open-Meteo or WeatherAPI.com. To get forecasts and find cities, our server sends the rounded coordinates and time zone of your city, and the text you type into the city search. The requests come from our server, so the provider does not receive your IP address, account number or any other identifier.
  • AI services: OpenRouter (OpenRouter, Inc.), which forwards each request to the AI model provider that processes it (for example, Anthropic). We use it for two things: suggesting item details from a clothing photo you upload, and writing the short note that explains an outfit. For the note we send only the weather and the names of the clothing items. No names, account numbers or other identifiers are sent with either request.
  • GitHub (GitHub, Inc.) hosts this website (see section 11).

We may also disclose data if the law requires it, to protect people's safety or our rights, or to a successor if our business is transferred. In a transfer, this policy would continue to apply to your data.

6. International transfers

Some of our providers are based outside the European Economic Area, for example in the United States or the United Kingdom. When personal data is transferred outside the EEA, we rely on a European Commission adequacy decision (such as those for the United Kingdom and for companies certified under the EU–US Data Privacy Framework) or on the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses. You can ask us for more information about these safeguards.

7. How long we keep data

  • Account, child profiles, settings, wardrobe, photos, outfits, feedback and usage events: for as long as your account exists. They are deleted when you use “Delete all data” (see section 8).
  • Sign-in sessions: a session expires after at most 180 days and is replaced each time the app refreshes it.
  • Weather forecasts: cached for each map area (about 10 × 10 km) for a few hours and shared by everyone in that area. They are not linked to you.
  • Technical logs: kept by our hosting provider for a limited period for security and troubleshooting.
  • Backups: deleted data may remain in backups kept by our hosting provider until they are overwritten in the normal backup cycle.
  • Purchases: Apple and Adapty keep transaction records under their own policies and legal obligations. When you delete your data, we keep only an anonymous note that a deletion happened, with no account number.

8. Deleting your data

In the app, go to Profile → Delete all data → Delete. This permanently and immediately deletes from our servers:

  • If you set up the family: your account and Apple sign-in link, your sign-in sessions, every child profile, the wardrobe and all clothing photos (including the image files), outfits, “Dressed!” marks and walk feedback, day plans, your city and walk settings, family invitations, and usage events linked to you or your family. Any other adult who joined your family loses access to it.
  • If you joined someone else's family: your account, Apple sign-in link, sign-in sessions and usage events linked to you. The family's child profiles, wardrobe and outfits stay with the parent who created the family, including feedback and “Dressed!” marks you added.

Deleting your data does not cancel a subscription. To stop being charged, cancel in your iPhone's Settings → your name → Subscriptions. Data held by Apple is managed by Apple; to stop using Sign in with Apple for Bundle Up, go to Settings → your name → Sign-In & Security → Sign in with Apple.

Using Bundle Up as a guest? Please use “Delete all data” before you sign out or remove the app. Guest accounts aren't linked to a name or email address, so once you have signed out we may have no way to tell which data is yours.

You can also ask us to delete your data, or copies of usage events held by our analytics provider, by writing to hi@bundleup.me.

9. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to:

  • access the personal data we hold about you and get a copy of it;
  • correct inaccurate data (most of it you can edit in the app yourself);
  • have your data erased;
  • restrict how we use your data;
  • receive the data you gave us in a portable format;
  • object to processing based on our legitimate interests, including the usage events;
  • lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. In Poland this is the President of the Personal Data Protection Office (Prezes Urzędu Ochrony Danych Osobowych, ul. Stawki 2, 00-193 Warszawa, uodo.gov.pl). You can also contact the authority where you live or work.

To use these rights, write to hi@bundleup.me. We will reply within one month. We may ask for information that helps us confirm the request comes from the account holder, for example details only the account holder would know.

If you live in the United States: we do not sell personal information and do not share it for cross-context behavioural advertising. You can ask to know, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you, and we won't treat you differently for doing so.

10. Security

Data travels between the app and our server over encrypted HTTPS connections. We store only a one-way hash of long-lived sign-in tokens, serve photos through links that expire after a short time, round city coordinates to about 10 km, limit repeated sign-in attempts, and restrict access to production systems to the people who run the app. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your data and will notify you and the authorities of a breach where the law requires.

11. This website

bundleup.me uses no cookies, analytics or tracking scripts, and its fonts and images are served from the site itself. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub processes visitors' IP addresses to deliver the pages and keep the service secure, as described in GitHub's Privacy Statement. If you email us, we use your email address and message only to reply and keep a record of the conversation.

12. Changes to this policy

We will update this policy when the app or the law changes. The current version always lives at bundleup.me/privacy, with its effective date at the top. If a change materially affects how we use your data, we will tell you in the app before it takes effect.
