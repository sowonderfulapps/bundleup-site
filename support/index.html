Common questions

How do I restore my purchase?

Open Pompom, go to Profile and tap Pompom Premium, then tap Restore in the top-right corner of the screen. Make sure your iPhone is signed in to the App Store with the same Apple ID you used to subscribe.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. On your iPhone, open Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Pompom → Cancel Subscription. You keep Premium until the end of the period you've already paid for.

Deleting the app doesn't cancel a subscription. For a refund, contact Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

How do I delete my data?

In Pompom, open Profile, tap Delete all data and confirm with Delete. This permanently removes your child's profile, clothes, photos and history from our servers. It can't be undone.

Using Pompom as a guest? Delete your data before you sign out or remove the app, because guest accounts aren't linked to an email we could find you by. Deleting data doesn't cancel a subscription. More details are in our Privacy Policy.

How do I change my city or walk time?

Open the Profile tab and tap City or Walk to choose a new city or time. The next outfit uses the forecast for your new place and hours.

How do I add a second parent?

Invite them from the Profile tab: send the invitation link and ask them to open it on their iPhone with Pompom installed. They join your family and see the same child, wardrobe and outfits, and Premium is shared with them.

I changed phones. Where's my wardrobe?

If you used Pompom as a guest, your data stays with the old phone's app. On the old phone, go to Profile and tap Sign in with Apple; then, on the new phone, choose I already have an account and sign in with the same Apple ID.

The outfit feels too warm or too cold

After the walk, tap Cold, Fine or Hot. Pompom learns whether your child tends to run warm or chilly and adjusts the next outfits. It also helps to check that your walk time and how your child goes out (stroller, on foot, or running and playing) are set correctly.
